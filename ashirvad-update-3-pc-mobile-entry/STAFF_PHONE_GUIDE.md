# Staff ke liye: phone pe Ashirvad app kaise lagayein

## Android (Chrome)
1. Chrome mein apni website kholo aur login karo.
2. Home pe **"Nayi Entry (Mobile + PC)"** dabao.
3. Upar right ⋮ (teen dots) → **"Add to Home screen"** / **"Install app"** → Add.
4. Ab phone pe **Ashirvad** ka icon aa gaya. Roz isi se kholo.

## iPhone (Safari)
1. Safari mein website kholo, login karo, "Nayi Entry" dabao.
2. Neeche **Share** (⬆ wala dabba) → **"Add to Home Screen"** → Add.

## Roz ka kaam
- Upar date dekho. ‹ › se din badlo.
- Neeche **GH / Restaurant / Office** chuno.
- **GH:** room dabao → mobile number → naam → rate → log → type → payment → **"theek hai"**. Saare rooms ke baad neeche **"Din submit karo"**.
- **Restaurant:** Cash, Card, PP, Computer amount bharo. Kharch/Upaad "+ Jodo" se. **Farak hara** ho tabhi submit hota hai.
- **Office:** Aavak / Javak "+ Jodo" se, phir Submit.
- Net chala jaaye ya phone band ho jaaye to ghabrao mat, bhara hua data phone mein bacha rehta hai. Net aane pe Submit karo.
- GH ek baar submit ho gaya to badal nahi sakte. Galti ho to owner/admin ko batao.
